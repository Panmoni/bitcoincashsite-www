## Summary

CME Group announced Bitcoin Cash futures, set to launch 19 October pending regulatory review. That is the biggest institutional signal BCH has had in years. On-chain, the chain did what it always does: about 12,000 transactions a day, for about a tenth of a cent each. The open question for the next two months is whether the May 2027 upgrade carries Faster Blocks.

## The ecosystem

Of 371 listed projects in the [directory](/directory): 288 alive, 26 stale, 24 dead, 33 unknown.

Dead means the site failed two daily checks in a row. Four domains that directories still link to now redirect to spam, a casino or a domain-sale page: the old Zapit, tipbitcoincash, CashShuffle and Satoshi's Angels sites. We moved them, and 27 other gone projects, to the [graveyard](/directory#graveyard). XDEFI (later Ctrl) wallet shut down on 19 August.

For tokens, see [TokenStork](https://tokenstork.com), which tracks every CashToken category.

## What happened

- **CME futures.** On 22 September CME Group said it plans to list Bitcoin Cash futures (250 BCH) and Micro Bitcoin Cash futures (25 BCH) from 19 October, pending regulatory review.
- **May 2027 upgrade.** Nothing is locked in yet. Lock-in for the 2027 cycle is expected around 15 November 2026. [Faster Blocks](/chips/chip-2025-03-faster-blocks) is the main candidate, and stakeholder statements are split. A year with no consensus change is possible. Follow it on the [2027 upgrade page](/upgrades/2027-05-upgrade).
- **The May 2026 upgrade is live.** Loops, functions, bitwise shifts and pay-to-script have been on mainnet since May. See [what changed](/upgrades/2026-05-upgrade).
