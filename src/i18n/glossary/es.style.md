Spanish style brief for bchworks.com (full guide: docs/i18n/es-style.md).

- Neutral Latin American Spanish. No Spain-only words: billetera (not monedero), computadora, celular, hacer clic; ustedes, never vosotros.
- Address the reader as tú, always: puedes, tu billetera, descarga. Never usted, never vos.
- Menu items, buttons and tabs: infinitive (Comprar BCH, Comparar billeteras). Calls to action inside prose: tú imperative (Consigue una billetera).
- Keep the English tone: plain, direct, honest about risk. Add no enthusiasm.
- Kept English terms take a fixed gender and an -s plural: el UTXO, los UTXOs, la mempool, el halving, la blockchain, el exchange, los tokens. Do not italicise them.
- Numbers: decimal comma (0,5 BCH; 1,5 %). Thousands point from five digits up (12.345); none for four digits (2048) or years. A no-break space (U+00A0) before % and before a currency symbol (1234,50 US$).
- Dates: 18 de noviembre de 2026; months and weekdays lowercase; short form day/month/year.
- Titles and headings in sentence case: capitalise only the first word and proper names.
- Always use opening ¿ and ¡. Curly quotes “…” in prose. No serial comma before y or o.
- Document titles of English sources stay in English. A translated quotation ends with (traducción nuestra).
